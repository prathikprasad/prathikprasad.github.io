Mixed Reality Game, May 2025

WTFire?!: throw your stress into a campfire in your room

Play on Meta Quest

Short Summary

Like smashing a PC, minus the PC. A virtual campfire appears on your real floor. Grab the objects floating around you, throw them in, and each one does something unexpected while your stress meter drains.

Role
Concept and development

Context
2-day WebXR workshop, team of 4

Platform
Meta Quest 3, mixed reality in the browser, no install

Tools
WebXR, Three.js, Meta IWSDK

1

Grab

Squeeze to grab what's in reach, or pull it to you with the trigger.

2

Throw

Real physics: it arcs, bounces off your furniture, lands in the fire.

3

Surprise

27 objects, 27 different reactions.

4

Relax

Every burn drains the stress meter. At zero the fire turns calm blue.

5

Interrupted

Someone walks in and yells. Stress goes back up.

What Happens When You Burn It

Outcome

Built in two days as a team of four, then rebuilt on Meta's Immersive Web SDK with 27 objects, physics throwing and room-aware surfaces. It runs straight from the Quest browser.
